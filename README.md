# MgSO4 AR Sim — Augmented Reality Simulation for Magnesium Sulfate Administration

An Android augmented-reality training app for nursing students, built for the research study
**"Augmented Reality Simulation for Magnesium Sulfate (MgSO₄) Administration"**
(Faculty of Nursing, Mansoura University).

The app places a 3D eclampsia bedside (patient, bed, monitor, IV pump, medication tray, urine bag,
oxygen mask, reflex hammer) on a real table or floor and guides the learner through a 22-step
clinical scenario: seizure first aid, safety checks, baseline assessment, loading and maintenance
dose preparation, infusion-pump programming, hourly monitoring, recognising magnesium toxicity
and giving calcium gluconate.

## Features
- **AR Clinical Simulation** – 22-step scored scenario on the 3D bedside with live vital signs.
- **Toxicity Explorer** – drag serum Mg from 4 to 16 mg/dL and watch reflexes, respirations and
  consciousness change on the patient.
- **Patellar reflex test** – tap the knee: the hip flexes, the lower leg hangs, and the kick
  strength follows the current magnesium level (brisk → absent).
- **Learn** – 10 topic cards (eclampsia, indications, dosing regimens, preparing 4 g of 20 %
  solution, monitoring, toxicity, AHRQ safety practices, delivery, refractory seizures, BP).
- **Dose Calculation Practice** – 10 randomised calculations with worked solutions.
- **Knowledge Test** – 20-question pre-test / post-test / practice modes.
- **Results & Data** – participant ID, group, scores and timings exported as CSV for SPSS.
- Works on most phones: ARCore (AR Foundation) surface tracking when available, printed-marker
  tracking (Vuforia) otherwise, and a 3D orbit view on devices without AR.

## Building
1. Install Unity **6000.6.5f1** with Android Build Support.
2. Vuforia Engine 11.4.4 is not included (licence + size). Download
   `com.ptc.vuforia.engine-11.4.4.tgz` from the Vuforia developer portal into
   `MgSO4_AR_Simulation/Packages/`.
3. Put your own Vuforia licence key in `MgSO4_AR_Simulation/Assets/Resources/VuforiaLicense.txt`
   (only needed for marker mode; surface AR and 3D view work without it).
4. Open the project in Unity and use the menu **MgSO4 AR → 2. Build Android APK**, or run
   `Tools/build_android.bat` (copy it next to the project folder first).

The latest APK is attached to the GitHub release.
